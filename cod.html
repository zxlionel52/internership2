Our Academic Structure

At GS Kabare, we are committed to providing a robust academic foundation aligned with the Rwanda Education Board (REB) standards. Our students are prepared to excel in national examinations and beyond.

Ordinary Level (S1 - S3)

Focusing on a broad range of core competencies to build a strong foundation.

  • • Mathematics & Sciences
  • • Languages (Kinyarwanda, English, French)
  • • Social Studies
  • • ICT & Computer Literacy

Advanced Level (S4 - S6)

Specialized subject combinations tailored for career paths.

  • • MCB (Math, Chem, Bio)
  • • PCM (Physics, Chem, Math)
  • • HEG (History, Econ, Geog)
  • • MEG (Math, Econ, Geog)

Examination Excellence

Our students consistently perform well in National Examinations managed by NESA. We offer intensive remedial classes and study groups to ensure every student reaches their target grade.
