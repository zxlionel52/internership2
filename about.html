Our Story

Established with the vision of nurturing academic brilliance and moral integrity, Excellence Academy has grown into one of the most respected secondary schools in Rwanda. Located in the heart of Nyarugenge, we serve students from all walks of life, providing a platform where innovation meets tradition.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education that inspires students to reach their full potential and contribute positively to the socio-economic development of Rwanda.

Our Vision

To be the premier center for academic excellence, innovation, and character building in East Africa.

Why Choose Us?

  • Aligned with NESA/REB Standards: We follow the official Rwandan national curriculum with advanced pedagogical techniques.
  • State-of-the-art Facilities: From our modern science laboratories to our digital libraries.
  • Holistic Development: We balance rigorous academics with sports, music, and community service.
  • Experienced Faculty: Our teachers are dedicated, qualified professionals committed to student mentorship.

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by our commitment to:

  • Integrity: Honesty in all academic and personal pursuits.
  • Unity (Ndi Umunyarwanda): Fostering a spirit of togetherness and national pride.
  • Excellence: Striving for the best in every task we undertake.
