Student Support Services at GS Kabare

Our Commitment to You

At GS Kabare, we understand that students face various challenges during their academic journey. Our dedicated support team ensures that every student has the tools and environment necessary to thrive academically, emotionally, and socially.

Academic Tutoring

We provide after-school remedial sessions for students who need extra help in subjects like Mathematics, Physics, and English to ensure no one is left behind.

Guidance & Counseling

Our school counselor offers a safe space for students to talk about their challenges, build confidence, and receive advice on personal growth and career paths.

Health & First Aid

We have a dedicated on-campus First Aid center equipped to handle minor medical needs and emergencies, ensuring the safety of all students during school hours.

Peer Mentorship

Through our "Big Brother/Big Sister" program, senior students mentor junior students, fostering a culture of unity, respect, and mutual support within the school.

Inclusive Education

We embrace the Rwandan value of "Ndi Umunyarwanda." We are committed to an inclusive environment where every student, regardless of their background or learning speed, is treated with dignity and provided with the specific support they need to succeed.
